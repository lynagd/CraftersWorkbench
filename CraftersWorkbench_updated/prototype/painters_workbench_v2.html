In real convolution terms

  • Glowing frame = the kernel, a small 3×3 filter that slides over the image
  • 9 tiles inside = the pixels under the kernel
  • Your tool = 9 weights (the grid). Each pixel is multiplied by its weight, then all 9 results are added up
  • One new tile = the output pixel
  • Whole pass = a convolution: the same kernel at every position makes a new image
